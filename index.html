Why frame the tradeoff

A benefit can be real without being the whole decision.

NIST’s Generative AI profile recommends governing, mapping, measuring, and managing risk across the AI lifecycle. Its AI Resource Center also emphasizes testing, evaluation, verification, and validation. A strong research question therefore asks what improves, what may degrade, and what evidence can distinguish the two.
